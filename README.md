# Simulators

A collection of free simulators that run right in your browser, plus Quasar, our AI assistant.

**Start here:** https://jacob711-oss.github.io/Simulators/

No install or account needed. Works on computers, tablets and phones.

## In every simulator
- **Works offline:** visit once online, and every simulator opens later without internet.
- **Free play with saved worlds:** create worlds, carry on later, and Save & exit while playing. Worlds are saved in your browser on that device.
- **Challenges:** goals from easy to fiendish, with stars to earn.
- **Tutorial and demo:** learn the controls step by step, or watch the highlights.
- **Snapshot and full screen** buttons next to the zoom controls.
- **Phone friendly:** touch controls, pinch to zoom, and layouts made for small screens.

## On the Our Simulations! page
- **Continue playing:** your latest saved worlds from every simulator, one tap to jump back in.
- **Search and categories:** Space, Life, Earth, Science, Transport, Sport and AI.
- **Share** buttons, picture link previews, and an installable home-screen icon.

## Reviews
Leave a review at the bottom of the [Our Simulations! page](https://jacob711-oss.github.io/Simulators/#review), or from the menu inside any simulator.

## How it works
Everything is plain files served by GitHub Pages; the simulations run entirely in your browser. Reviews are sent by FormSubmit, Quasar uses the free Pollinations AI service, its web search uses Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo, and its videos come from Wikimedia Commons.
