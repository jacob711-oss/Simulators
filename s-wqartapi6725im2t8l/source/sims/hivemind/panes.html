Views

Ants are almost blind. A searching ant leaves "home" scent behind it; an ant carrying food leaves "food" scent. Searchers follow food scent, carriers follow home scent, and busy routes turn into highways. Big colonies can raid their rivals: a war party marches on the enemy nest, fights its way in and carries off food. A colony with no ants and no food falls.
